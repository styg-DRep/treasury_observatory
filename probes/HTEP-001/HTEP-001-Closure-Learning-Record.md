## 2. Return to the activated agreement

**Original driver.** Historical treasury activity may contain useful evidence about costs, estimates, funding trajectories and delivery, but it is scattered across mechanisms and records. Before a larger evidence system is justified, a bounded sample should test whether such records can become durable, reusable evidence supporting longitudinal learning and responsible cost inquiry.

**Local hypotheses:** P1 reusable evidence; P2 minimal normalization; P3 longitudinal value; P4 cost comparability; P5 proportionate intake.

**Primary parent hypotheses:** H01 durable shared memory, H02 longitudinal integration, H06 historical reference classes, H11 proportionate burden. **Secondary:** H12 commons capacity rather than institutional dependency.

**Working principle:** “Collect once where practical, preserve provenance, interpret cautiously, and leave the evidence more reusable than you found it.”

The ~30 historical actions/proposals in the initial plan were a purposive **methodological target**, not a statistical quota. The activated agreement expressly allows a *documented early stopping decision*. HTEP was not authorized to build a complete database, enforce a final taxonomy, rate proposers, establish binding cost rules, or determine the final Observatory architecture.

## 3. What was conducted

Reconnaissance established a reproducible Koios governance-action snapshot and the 107-action TWD population, investigated budget-selection pathways and constraints, explored source-backed TWD relationships, and surveyed Catalyst funding rounds and evidence regimes. Sampling proceeded in two gated phases: S1–S4, checkpoint, then S5–S6, checkpoint. A potential S7 was deliberately not activated. The Phase 2 [S5 memo](Phase_2/S5-Liqwid-Fund2-Bounded-Intake.md), [S6 memo](Phase_2/S6-Orion-Xverse-Bounded-Intake.md), [learning checkpoint](Phase_2/phase2-learning-checkpoint-v0.1.md), and [selection log](htep-sample-frame-v0.2.md) are committed as companion evidence.

| **S2 — Eternl** | Earlier funding, an expired request and a revised enacted request; exchange-rate assumptions alter ADA amounts without equivalent change in planned USD cost | Proposal characterization, governance vote evidence and realized spending are distinct |

The six histories are **deliberately contrasting case studies**, not a representative sample from which population rates or causal outcome claims can be estimated. The original probe §5 variation dimensions (funding size, project type, period, recurrence, maintenance/development, incumbent/first-time and technical/non-technical) were re-specified **before sampling** in Sample Frame v0.1 around funding instrument, governance/selection pathway, temporal relations, evidence maturity and cost semantics; Sample Frame v0.2 retained the deliberate contrast. This was a documented conduct-stage selection decision, not an after-the-fact adjustment to rationalize stopping. **Uncovered original dimensions** include non-technical work (all six cases concern technical development or financial allocation) and a systematic incumbent-versus-first-time-recipient contrast. The sample therefore cannot support representativeness claims along those axes.

## 4. What changed in our understanding

1. **The durable commonality is evidentiary practice, not one homogeneous treasury-project row.** Requests, grants, withdrawn funds, contingent reserves, financial interests, returns, recipient payments, delivery and institutional acceptance carry different economic and evidentiary meanings.
2. **Relationships are source-backed assertions.** Continuation, revision, prior-funding reference, shared contributors and downstream investment participation are distinguishable; one source's attestation does not independently verify causal continuity or cash movement.
3. **Administrative richness and cost-verification richness do not move together.** A mature milestone trail can explain what an institution accepted without providing a verified realized-cost denominator. Older proposals may expose more interpretable planned labor assumptions.
4. **Historical comparisons can be useful precisely when their limits are visible.** Identifying false equivalences—exchange rates, contingencies, proposal vs award, public vs confidential deployment—is decision-relevant, but it is not a verified reference class.
5. **Preservation means both integrity and substantive completeness.** A valid checksum of an HTTP response does not mean the substantive record was captured. Fund 14's Milestone Module shell remains an openly declared limitation accepted during Corpus review.
6. **Contributor capacity is an empirical context, not a free-standing value claim.** Early grant intentions included skills and future productive capability. Funding histories involving development groups may warrant later inquiry, but this probe did not prove causal development effects.
7. **Stopping is a form of stewardship.** The early stop after six cases was made at learning gates, not by exhaustion or neglect. It reduces research effort, review burden, interpretive overclaiming and long-term Corpus maintenance obligations.

## 6. Parent formation-hypothesis learning

- **H01 — Durable Shared Memory:** Evidence records survive in the separate Corpus with provenance, reproducible Koios extracts, native Catalyst IDs and known limitations. This supports feasibility, not proof of permanence or universal reusability.
- **H02 — Longitudinal Integration:** Clear exploratory support: source-backed relationships reveal meaningful histories otherwise hidden in isolated proposal/actions.
- **H06 — Historical Reference Classes:** **Constrain** the ambition. Start from comparable economic objects, denominators and verification grades; do not turn plans or awards into realized-cost benchmarks. Later prospective capture or external comparables might be worthwhile, but are not authorized here.
- **H11 — Proportionate Burden:** Qualitative support for stopping discipline and AI-assisted stewardship under review, **not** confirmation of the probe's prospective time logging. The **separate Section 11 Burden Record v0.1** records the steward-reported **16 hours**, **41 commits**, **6 PRs**, qualitative friction, and unmeasured reviewer/compute/maintenance effort. These counts describe the recorded period, not a guaranteed final Git history total or a system-wide cost model.
- **H12 — Commons Capacity vs Dependency:** Independent reviewers improved provenance, portability, shared relationship vocabulary and source-completeness claims without adopting Observatory judgments. This is an early institutional signal; a genuine third-party cold-start reinterpretation remains untested.

## 8. Pathmap decision and competing branches

**Decision:** HTEP has generated enough *potentially decision-relevant* contrasts to justify **proposing**, but not automatically activating, a bounded Assessment Prototype. It has **not** demonstrated that actual participants regard the evidence as decision-useful. Pathmap §7 treats participant-judged relevance as a learning signal, not something already established by the investigator.

### Branches reached and not selected now

- **Branch A — longitudinal usefulness:** Source-backed repeated and delegated histories provide early support for improving evidentiary objects, but not a mandate for broad corpus expansion or authoritative reference classes.
- **Branch B — sparse but recoverable sources: partly reached.** Missing early reports, pointer-only Orion evidence and the Catalyst Milestone Module shell support later exploration of minimal prospective capture and resilient preservation. **Defer** that branch because the available evidence is sufficient for a small presentation test and the accepted gaps do not presently obstruct the next question.
- **Branch C — poor realized-cost comparability: reached for this sample.** HTEP did not establish a matched verified realized-cost reference class. H06's competing explanation—**external market comparables or bottom-up estimation may need to remain primary**—now carries additional weight. **Redirect** any cost-reference ambition toward prospective calibration and/or external comparators in a separately authorized future inquiry. Do not fabricate a historical benchmark to satisfy an assessment format.
- **Branch D — anchoring:** Not yet observed with participants; a prospective representation experiment can test it. This also engages **H13, reflexive detection of Observatory-induced distortion**.

### Why an Assessment Prototype remains defensible

The *case for* moving to presentation is that false economic equivalences become particularly consequential when displayed as neat comparisons. The *case against* is that the weakest results—quantified burden (H11) and cold-start independent reuse (H12)—remain untested; assessment interfaces risk scaling unexamined maintenance and authority. The next experiment should therefore address both rather than claiming they have already been solved.

A **candidate, separate** Assessment Prototype would invite a small number of voluntary readers to use **only existing Corpus materials**, with no Observatory case memos supplied initially. Compare raw provenance-linked evidence, lightly structured observations with visible uncertainty, and (if justified) differing explicitly attributed interpretations of the *same* source. Record participants' ability to distinguish observed from inferred claims, decision relevance as they themselves judge it, anchoring or false certainty, use of provenance, and a **prospective effort log** (preparation, participant/reviewer time and recurring interpretation burden). Do not force a participant to issue a funding recommendation.

The Pathmap's original **reference-class comparison** and **composite confidence indicator** presentation candidates are **deliberately omitted from the first prototype**, because P4 has not supplied an adequate realized-cost basis and composite authority risks would confound the first test. They may be reconsidered only if later evidence and a separately reviewed mandate justify them. The accurate registered H10 name is **Cost Assurance Can Remain Distinct from Value Judgment**.

**Authorization gate:** Propose a new issue/PR specifying driver, participants, evidence boundaries, ethics, minimal measures and stopping rules. This closure only changes HTEP's state and informs the Pathmap; it does not activate the Assessment Prototype.

## 10. Five-question final learning record

**What did we expect?** A bounded historical sample might produce reproducible evidence, useful common structure, longitudinal insight, some cost-reference learning and a proportionate intake practice.

**What did we observe?** Six contrasting histories and reviewed Corpus handoffs; strong lessons about epistemic and financial distinctions; longitudinal information; no verified realized-cost reference class; incomplete source bodies in specific systems; meaningful peer corrections.

**What changed?** Evidence stewardship is better understood as preserving provenance, qualified claims and multiple relationships rather than homogenizing all funding into one project schema. Small definitions and identifiers can be useful without institutionalizing an ontology. Stopping and independent review are conditions of legitimacy, not merely process overhead.

**What remains unresolved?** Verified realized expenditure, causal development of contributor capacity, complete public recovery of some older/dynamic sources, and quantitative long-term operating costs.

**What should happen next?** Accept closure with qualified findings, keep the Section 11 burden record independent, and open a separate, bounded Assessment Prototype formation proposal if the community judges the next uncertainty worth investigating.
