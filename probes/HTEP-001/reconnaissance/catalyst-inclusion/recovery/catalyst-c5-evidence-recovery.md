# HTEP-001 — Catalyst C5: Four-era Evidence Recovery Trial (v0.1)

**Research date:** 2026-11-03. **Status:** bounded observational test; three project-level records recovered, one early cohort investigated at round/category level only. **Parent:** HTEP Catalyst C1/C3 landscape v0.1. This is an Observatory research artifact, not a Corpus source snapshot.

## Driver and decision boundary

Determine whether historical Catalyst project records across four distinct eras support reusable, provenance-aware evidence at a burden proportionate to HTEP, with specific attention to whether formal milestone and compliance processes made *publicly reusable* evidence better, worse, or merely different. Do **not** evaluate project merit, infer participant motives, or equate administrative approval with independently verified value.

**Source status legend:** **Observed** = public source displays the relevant field or text; **Referenced** = the record links to another artifact but the artifact itself was not independently checked; **Unresolved** = not established; **Not expected** = an instrument belonging to another era or process, not a record failure.

## Case selection and results

### E1 — Early grant cohort, Fund 2–4 (early-boundary test; project case not closed)

- **Grounded cohort:** Fund 2, `https://projectcatalyst.io/funds/2` and its funded-proposal category `https://projectcatalyst.io/funds/2/challenge`.
- **Observed:** Fund 2 reports 11 funded proposals, 9 marked complete, 2 did not finish, USD 200,732 total shown as requested and distributed. The category page preserves the original question around developer and entrepreneur adoption, with voting and review context.
- **Not established in this pass:** a particular funded Fund 2, 3, or 4 grant with a stable individual proposal URL and recoverable project budget/delivery record. Search and official category navigation surfaced round-level records, but not a verified individual early project record. Consequently **E1 cannot be scored as a fully recovered project case**. This is an actual retrieval limitation encountered, not evidence of absent early project data.
- **Meaning:** oldest-round aggregate discovery is feasible; individual early proposal identification, immutable versions, and proof of completion remain unverified. The Fund 4 category pages show monthly-report-era institutional context, but do not substitute for a project-level recovery.
- **Reopen only if:** HTEP's first sample requires a Fund 2–4 case, or the recoverability boundary directly changes the sample strategy. A targeted route via a publicly queryable historical proposal index may be more effective than a general web search.

### E2 — Expansion era, Fund 8, “Universal Resolver for did:prism”

- **Official proposal:** `https://projectcatalyst.io/funds/8/accelerate-decentralized-identity/universal-resolver-for-didprism`; site ID **800352**.
- **Observed:** marked Complete, funded/requested and distributed **USD 6,425**, no remaining amount; reported Yes/No voting totals; original problem, proposed solution, feasibility schedule, deliverables and budget breakdown. Development budget expressly calculated as 40 hours at USD 100/hr + 20 hours at USD 100/hr + USD 400 hosting + USD 25 domain/SSL. Reports dated May, June and July 2022 with links to external documents, including a final report.
- **Referenced, not independently checked:** linked Google Drive reports, the public code and hosting deliverables, and linked prior F6/F7 initiatives. The project says it is not a continuation but situates itself in the proposer's broader related work; these are not equivalent assertions.
- **Cost-value utility:** usable **planned** labor rates, duration, categories and deliverables; reported grant distribution; not verified actual hours, actual expenditure, or persistent output quality.
- **Interpretation:** this case provides unusually good cost structure without a modern Milestone Module record. Reporting mechanism is monthly narrative, not SoM/PoA approval sequences.

### E3 — Mature milestone era, Fund 11, “Cardanoscan API Javascript SDK”

- **Milestone Module:** `https://milestones.projectcatalyst.io/projects/1100083/milestones`; project ID **1100083**.
- **Observed:** project title, category F11: Cardano Open: Developers, **ADA 64,000** budget and distributed, start date March 11, 2024, Completed stage, milestone 1 output (support for four API groups) and timestamped milestone submission; milestone review/PoA structure visible through the module. Fund 11 rules establish grant-only and milestone-based reporting and review expectations.
- **Caution:** public module pages show a generic-seeming discrepancy: **Statement of Milestones allocation ADA 0** vs **project budget ADA 64,000**, despite milestone records appearing. This is an observed interface/integrity warning; no claim is made that the actual underlying grant or payouts were zero.
- **Unresolved:** original proposal text and vote record linked to this specific milestone ID, detailed public review histories, independent deliverable inspection, actual expenses and chain disbursement verification.
- **Cost-value utility:** project amount and milestone-level scopes and approvals, but not safely normalized into realized labor-unit prices without more evidence.

### E4 — Late milestone/compliance era, Fund 14, “Hydra-Point-of-Sale: Accept ADA, CNTs in Retail & Invoicing”

- **Milestone Module:** `https://milestones.projectcatalyst.io/projects/1400065`; project ID **1400065**.
- **Observed:** F14 Cardano Open: Developers; budget and reported distributed **ADA 95,000**; start November 24, 2025; Completed; multiple milestone records with costs, schedules, payment plans, reviewer approval/refusal counts, PoA timestamps, and admin-team sign-off. The first milestone recap shows cost ADA 25,000, two approvals, zero refusals, and a PoA administrative sign-off on January 21, 2026.
- **Important discrepancy:** module simultaneously says Statement of Milestones allocated **ADA 0**, inconsistent with grant budget **ADA 95,000** and visible milestone figures. Treat as a display/data reconciliation issue requiring targeted follow-up only if selected for ingestion.
- **Process context (not a finding about this project's reviewers):** Fund 13 documentation required KYC/KYB/AML due diligence for grantees, while Fund 14 reviewer registration documentation required completed KYC by the registration deadline. Later Fund 14 rules impose participant-role disclosure, eligibility checks, milestone verification and possible administrative sanction. These sources document policy conditions, not the private verification records or motives of specific participants.
- **Unresolved:** identity verification events for this project (not suitable for public harvesting), original award-version snapshot, actual expenditure, and whether the more regulated framework improved downstream decisions or displaced useful public evidence.

## C5 evaluation against HTEP P1–P5

- **P1 Reusable evidence:** provisionally supported in F8, F11, and F14. Official project identifiers, public scopes, administrative statuses and reports provide repeated evidence handles. Earliest fund case remains a recovery gap.
- **P2 Minimal normalization:** supported in **layered** form: record `award amount + native currency + reported distribution + evidence kind + fund + project ID`; do not make monthly progress and milestone approval events equivalent data types.
- **P3 Longitudinal value:** promising but **not verified here**. F8 explicitly points to earlier related work; a cross-round project-level identity linkage requires a separate case.
- **P4 Cost comparisons:** tentative. F8 offers concrete planned labor-hour and budget rates; F11/14 offer milestone envelopes and acceptance structures. These are *different cost representations* and cannot be treated as interchangeable unit prices. None establishes actual expenditure.
- **P5 Proportionate burden:** public source recovery of three examples feasible; original proposal-to-milestone joins, outside evidence links, and early project discovery are the most visible points of friction. Investigating any of these across all 2,221 proposals would exceed present need.

## Process interpretation: accountability versus reusable evidence

Historical rules document an institutional transition: monthly progress reporting was introduced in Fund 4, milestone processes piloted in Fund 9, and by Fund 10 all projects entered the milestone-only model; monthly reports stopped being required from January 2024. Fund 13/14 documentation shows increased formal due diligence and participation controls. These facts do **not** prove that these changes caused better or worse project outcomes, fewer participants, or lower data quality.

The late-era structured records improve event traceability (specific milestone, review count, sign-off time), **while the recurring displayed ADA 0 SoM allocation warning shows why administrative detail alone is insufficient evidence of consistent financial data**. Private KYC/KYB records are neither an appropriate Corpus target nor an observable measure of funding success. Their publicly documented eligibility implications may nevertheless matter to the historical decision context.

## Stopping rule and disposition

**C5 is complete enough to inform HTEP case selection, with a declared partial recovery on the early-era case.** We have demonstrated contrasting reporting regimes and recoverable cost/evidence attributes, plus a potentially consequential data-consistency warning. Do not scrape all grants or adjudicate the participant rule changes as part of this survey.

**One optional, bounded follow-up:** recover **one** genuine Fund 2–4 project record with verified identity and at least proposal/budget/outcome information if HTEP sampling actually needs an early-fund case; otherwise preserve this failed-recovery observation as a negative result. Do not force a fourth complete project record through guesswork.
