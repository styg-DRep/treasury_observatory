## Discriminating question
How far back can historical Catalyst proposal, allocation, cost, and delivery-related claims be recovered and independently referenced, before standardized milestone reporting?

## Recoverable observations
The original Fund 2 request states **USD 38,500**; the later Catalyst archive reports **USD 41,195 allocated and distributed**. These are different reported financial observations and are **not reconciled**. The initial budget describes contractor development (USD 20,000), specialist work (USD 16,000), advisory/data-science allowance (~USD 2,000), and taxes/transaction costs (USD 500). A specialist ten-day estimate at USD 800/day is not arithmetically explained by the USD 16,000 allocation. The subsequent Fund 3 proposal **300015** references this earlier funding and reports work in progress; it is an attributed project account, not independently verified delivery or expenditure. An original closeout link was identified but not recovered.

## Epistemic and cost boundary
A detailed *proposed* contractor budget cannot establish realized contractor rates, hours, expenditure, or technical outcomes. The present archive's “complete” designation is an administrative status. The initial intention to develop contributor expertise is documented, but the longer-term capacity effect was not causally evaluated.

## P1–P5 and stopping
P1: partial positive for recoverable historical evidence; P2: existing event/claim distinctions suffice; P3: later proposal references the earlier award; P4: useful *planned* cost categories, not realized-cost reference data; P5: retrieval failures recorded, no reliable task-level time log. **Close S5**: the early historical boundary was tested; pursuing closeout documents or financial reconciliation would be a separately justified inquiry.
