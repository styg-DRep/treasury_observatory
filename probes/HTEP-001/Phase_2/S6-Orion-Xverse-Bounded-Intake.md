## Learning and stop
This is a new financial topology for HTEP: an identifiable downstream recipient may coexist with an unobservable public transfer amount. P1: qualified source-backed record; P2: a local external-assertion vocabulary suffices, no premature cross-institution schema; P3: institution-to-recipient relation made visible; P4: realized investment cost **unavailable**, no fabricated zero or inferred allocation; P5: further confidential-investment reconstruction would exceed this intake's remit. **Close S6** and **do not activate S7** unless a material new hypothesis requires it.
