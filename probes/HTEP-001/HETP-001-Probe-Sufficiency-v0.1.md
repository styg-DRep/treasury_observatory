# HTEP-001 — Probe-Level Sufficiency Review v0.1

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** Proposed `check → learn` record; **not** a probe closure or amendment  
**Basis:** Activated Historical Treasury Evidence Probe as reproduced in *Cost Verification Framework* (sections 1–15); Sample Frame v0.2; Phase 1 and Phase 2 learning checkpoints; S1–S6 bounded intake records; associated reconnaissance artifacts.  
**Decision proposed:** End historical sampling now; finish a small amount of already-agreed evidence-preservation and learning documentation; then close HTEP with qualified findings.

## 1. Return to the probe driver

The original probe asks whether a bounded, heterogeneous sample of historical treasury evidence can become **reusable evidence** that supports **longitudinal insight** and a **limited test of cost comparison**, at **proportionate burden**, *before* larger evidence infrastructure is designed. Its domain includes collecting and preserving evidence, experimental normalization, exploratory analysis and reporting the burden. It expressly excludes a complete database, final cost taxonomy, definitive pricing judgment, proposer scores, binding benchmarks or final Observatory architecture.

The probe's operative principle is: **“Collect once where practical, preserve provenance, interpret cautiously, and leave the evidence more reusable than you found it.”**

The right test is therefore not “Did six cases establish treasury cost effectiveness?” nor “Did we process approximately 30 rows?” It is: **What can be faithfully reused, what has been learned about how evidence must be handled, and is that learning sufficient to inform the Observatory's next formation decision?**

## 2. What we expected, what we observed, and what changed

| Expectation | Observation from HTEP | Change in understanding |
|---|---|---|
| Historical evidence could be collected consistently | Source-backed claims recovered from six distinct histories, but source capture/archiving varies | Consistency is possible in provenance and claim discipline, not necessarily in a universal project row |
| A light common schema might enable comparison | Event, relationship, amount-kind, claim, source and assessment distinctions recur; regimes differ deeply | A **thin evidentiary grammar** may be sufficient; do not assume one ontology for all projects |
| Linking funding records yields longitudinal insight | Amaru continuation; Eternl revision; Liqwid later proposal testimony; milestone sequences; Orion delegated investment | Relationships should be evidenced assertions; neither shared identity nor adjacency alone proves continuity or causation |
| Some historical costs would support comparison | Planned labor/rate detail in Fund 2 and Fund 8; currency-repricing and contingency meaning in TWDs; no verified matched realized-cost pair | Proposed costs and budget semantics are available; independent *realized cost reference classes* have **not** been demonstrated |
| Intake might be proportionate | Six cases closed at useful boundaries; many dead ends and ambiguities preserved rather than pursued | Marginal-learning stopping proved workable qualitatively; actual effort/cost was not reliably measured |

Unexpected observations: institutional assessment differs from independent verification; greater procedural formalization can improve traceability without increasing realized-cost comparability; delegated investment can expose a named recipient while financially shielding deployment details; early funding proposals could explicitly seek to develop contributor capability, beyond an individual artifact. No causal contributor-capacity benefit was established.

All findings are methodological observations from a **purposive six-history sample**; not prevalence estimates, causal analyses or a cost-effectiveness verdict on Cardano funding.

## 4. Original probe closure conditions: current evidence

1. **Sample processed or reason for stopping:** **Met.** Six intentionally contrasting histories, two learning checkpoints, and documented diminishing marginal learning. Original approximately-30 target was methodological forecast rather than statistical sampling mandate; explain deviation explicitly in closure PR.
2. **Corpus records committed:** **Not demonstrated for all six sampled histories.** The Koios snapshot and derived TWD inventory exist; case memos and source links alone are not the promised independent Corpus sample. Do not call this condition satisfied based on Observatory files.
3. **Analytical outputs available:** **Met.** Reconnaissance, TWD relationship register, Catalyst landscape, S1–S6 case memos, phase checkpoints, cost-object distinctions and longitudinal representations.
4. **Limitations and ambiguity documented:** **Met.** Missing reports, conflicting figures, unverified disbursements, confidential investment terms, institutional vs independent verification and causal boundaries are visible.
5. **Burden recorded:** **Partial.** Qualitative friction and stop decisions exist. The original probe requests time locating, extracting and normalizing. Do not fabricate past minutes. A closure statement should explicitly mark elapsed effort as **not reliably collected**, and note what prospective logging would require. Whether that is an acceptable variance is a closure-review decision.
6. **Parent hypotheses updated:** **Partial.** Local P1–P5 analyses exist, but H01/H02/H06/H11 and secondary H12 should receive explicit learning update using the table above.
7. **Next recommended path explicit:** **Partial.** This memo proposes closure after two narrowly bounded completion tasks and identifies separate future inquiry paths; final path belongs in accepted closure learning record.

Interpretation criteria from original probe: evidence usefulness, independent reusability, comparability limits, proportional burden, epistemic integrity, and unexpected learning. Positive evidence exists for insight and integrity; independent reuse and measured burden remain weakest.

## 5. The material distinction: epistemic sufficiency versus contractual completion

**Epistemic sufficiency to stop sampling:** Yes. Another general case is unlikely to change the next formation decision proportionately. S7 should remain unactivated. A nonresult on P4 is legitimate and should not trigger an indefinite hunt for realized costs.

**Sufficiency to close the activated probe as agreed:** Not quite. The Corpus deliverable and an explicit parent-hypothesis learning record are part of the original agreement, not optional embellishments. They can be completed without collecting another historical history.

Avoid a false choice between “declare complete” and “build a comprehensive system.” The minimal remaining work is to discharge the original evidence/learning commitments at the smallest independently defensible scope.

## 6. Bounded closure-completion proposal (not a new research phase)

**A — Corpus evidence handoff / independent reuse check.** Choose a **small representative subset** (e.g. one Koios-linked TWD history and one Catalyst case) for demonstration of source/provenance records and minimally normalized, version-labelled observations. For the other sampled cases, provide explicit canonical IDs, source registers and preservation/access limitations, or else record why complete corpus preservation is not feasible under the probe. This must be transparently reviewed as possible *partial fulfillment* of the original requirement (“every proposal ... durable evidence record”), not silently treated as full compliance. Use original source or source identifiers; never commit unverified interpretive judgments as canonical facts. Ask whether a reader outside the Observatory can independently trace and reuse the records without adopting case conclusions. Stop after this demonstrator and an explicit inventory of what remains missing.

**B — Parent learning + burden caveat.** Submit H01/H02/H06/H11 and H12 updates, documented non-positive P4 finding, an honest burden limitation (“qualitative friction logged; elapsed time not measured”), sample-size stopping rationale, branches, and reopen triggers. This uses existing evidence, not more intake.

**Closure gate:** Review the remaining Corpus gap against original agreement. If a short, truthful representative handoff and transparent deviations are accepted as sufficient, close the probe with **qualified findings** via review PR. If the original per-case durable record promise is held strictly, create only the minimum six case-level provenance/record entries; do not build universal infrastructure or more research cases to meet it. Do not close before a documented decision.

## 7. Formation consequences and future branches

1. **Near-term formation emphasis:** Minimal provenance-first evidence grammar + source preservation + explicit claim status; preserve plural interpretations and no ratings or pricing authority.
2. **Focused cost-comparability probe (new, if justified):** Matched work scope, denomination/time, units, and verification; may test planned-cost reference classes, external estimates, or prospective actuals. Do not advertise realized cost benchmarks from HTEP.
3. **Prospective capture / burden experiment (new):** Identify what low-cost source capture, versioning and effort logging would improve; treat source retrieval effort as a first-class constraint.
4. **Representation/assessment prototype (next Pathmap possibility):** Test how audiences read evidence, uncertainty, and competing interpretations before conferring analytical authority.
5. **Contributor capability / TxPipe relationships:** Preserve as a bounded research lead about what spending is intended to sustain. Do not treat capacity, value or treasury causality as proven or mandate impact metrics.

These are **options**, not a new plan or preauthorizations. The Observatory's domain purpose is strengthened by making historical memory usable without monopolizing the right to interpret it.

## 8. Proposed closure learning record

- **What did we expect?** A ~30-case bounded sample could help test reusable normalized evidence, longitudinal usefulness and cost reference classes without unjustified burden.
- **What did we observe?** Six carefully contrasted histories and broad reconnaissance revealed reusable claim-level evidence, longitudinal links and a diverse financial/administrative topology, with source-preservation, actual-cost and effort gaps.
- **What changed?** The durable unit is more plausibly a provenance-bearing observation/relationship than a one-row homogeneous funding proposal. Financial meaning and verification type are indispensable. Sampling has reached diminishing returns.
- **What remains unresolved?** Full independent Corpus reuse, matched realized-cost comparisons, quantitative burden, contributor-capacity attribution, private investment flows and broader representativeness.
- **What next?** Stop sampling; perform minimal Corpus handoff and parent-hypothesis learning update; then review qualified closure. Branch remaining questions into future scoped experiments only when decision-relevant.

## 9. Current decision

**Recommend: stop further HTEP sampling; finish bounded closure obligations; then `check → learn → close` with qualified findings.** No S7, no ~30-case quota, no new full ontology or institution, no assertion that a proven cost-comparison service now exists.

This memo is a **review draft**. It does not change the activated probe or claim any GitHub commit, acceptance, or closure has occurred.
