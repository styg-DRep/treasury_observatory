## Evidence-system findings

**Positive:** Acceptance criteria and intended proof were written ahead of review; four separately dated administrative approval events survive; Fund 14 policy identifies two reviewer approvals and administrative sign-off as gates for staged grant disbursement. A later reader can distinguish proposal, milestone, evidence acceptance, and public completion state.

**Boundary:** The Milestone Module simultaneously shows `Statement of Milestones allocated ADA 0.00` error and four milestone costs totaling ADA 95,000. This appears inconsistent as a *displayed state*. It does not prove a zero-value Statement of Milestones, absence of funding, or project wrongdoing. Record as unresolved source/presentation inconsistency.

**Limits:** The public label 'completed' and two reviewer approvals are evidence of an institutional adjudication, not independent verification of functionality, real-world adoption, capital spending, or benefit. The promised repo, merchant pilots, release, and closeout metrics were not audited here. The final Google Drive report could not be read via the available search fetch. Without transaction records, distributed ADA 95,000 is an administrator-reported amount rather than independently reconciled receipt.

**Compliance context:** Fund 14 published KYC completion as an eligibility prerequisite for community reviewers and extensive funded-proposer onboarding rules. This establishes formal participation constraints but not their effect on who participated, verification quality, project selection, or public data quality. We should not collect private KYC documents; nor equate platform participation controls with stronger public cost evidence.

## Provisional HTEP signals

- **P1 reusable evidence:** Positive for proposals, milestone budgets, acceptance decisions and sign-off events; qualified by mutable pages and unretrieved artifacts.
- **P2 minimal normalization:** Feasible for provenance-based event records; brittle if attempt to force all submissions into one project 'cost' field.
- **P3 longitudinal learning:** Supports intra-project proposed-to-approved tracking; evidence of post-award outcomes not independently established.
- **P4 cost comparison:** Stage/role budget categorization available; no hours, actual costs, or verified productivity values; not directly rate-comparable to S3.
- **P5 proportional burden:** Public recap already answers administrative-status question. Technical assessment or ledger disbursement reconciliation requires new, separately justified work.

S4 intake **complete enough** for Phase 1 synthesis.
