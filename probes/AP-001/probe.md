# AP-001 — Evidence Representation Prototype

**Status:** Proposed — active only if this agreement is reviewed and merged  
**Formation issue:** [#7](https://github.com/styg-DRep/treasury_observatory/issues/7)  
**Predecessor:** HTEP-001 (closed, qualified findings)  
**Stewardship object:** Treasury Observatory methods and learning; source evidence remains in the independent Treasury Evidence Corpus.

## 1. Driver and consequential uncertainty

The Treasury Observatory explores whether durable, shared and contestable evidence can improve treasury sensemaking while preserving plural judgment. HTEP-001 recovered useful histories but did not establish verified matched realized-cost reference classes, independent cold-start usefulness, or quantified transferable intake costs.

**Question:** Can transparent, uncertainty-aware representations of the *same independently preserved historical evidence* improve participant understanding and traceability without creating false comparability, anchoring or de facto assessment authority?

This is a representation experiment, **not** a test or authorization of prospective proposer intake. History is used to investigate the conditions for current judgment, not to relitigate earlier funding decisions.

## 2. Domain and exclusions

AP-001 may produce small alternative evidence views, collect voluntary reader interpretations, observe harms and burden, and leave reviewable learning. It may not create proposal ratings, rankings, funding recommendations, production tooling, binding cost rules, unverified reference-class comparisons, composite confidence indicators, or prospective intake requirements. It does not change any Corpus schema or grant the Observatory authority over Corpus records.

## 3. Formation hypotheses

Primary: **H03** plural interpretation; **H04** method transparency; **H07** explicit uncertainty; **H10** separation of cost assurance from value judgment. Cross-cutting: **H11** proportionate burden; **H12** capacity rather than dependency; **H13** reflexive detection of Observatory-induced distortion.

Local learning questions:

- **P1 — Comprehension:** Compared with source-first reading, do structured views help readers correctly distinguish observations, transformations, assertions, missingness and uncertainty? Misclassification or increased unwarranted confidence counts against P1.
- **P2 — Traceability and plurality:** Can readers locate supporting sources and articulate differing defensible interpretations without adopting an Observatory conclusion? Failure to trace consequential claims or convergence induced by presentation complicates P2.
- **P3 — False comparison and authority:** Do any views imply equivalence between unlike economic events, or induce readers to treat layout or Observatory authorship as an approval signal? Evidence of either counts against the method.
- **P4 — Reuse and burden:** Can a cold-start reader work from the Corpus without an investigator-led explanation, at effort proportionate to participant-judged benefit? An inability to do so or excessive effort weakens the case for further work.

## 4. Sample and materials

Use exactly two contrasting HTEP histories from the existing Corpus:

- **S1 Amaru:** continuation, conditional contingency, reported returns, and distinctions among request, allocation and realized flows.
- **S2 Eternl:** revision and currency-repricing history; distinguish requested amounts, expired and enacted actions, and continuity claims.

The two histories are **contrasts, not a cost reference class**. No direct comparison of their project efficiency, realized costs, or merits is authorized.

For each case produce three *source-identical* variants:

1. **Source-first:** Corpus source/provenance links and minimal navigation without Observatory case-memo conclusions.
2. **Structured:** attributed facts, economic meanings, relationships, missingness, and visible uncertainty with traceable citations.
3. **Plural:** the same structured evidence plus at least two clearly attributed or explicitly hypothetical, non-authoritative alternative readings where supported by the record.

Do not introduce additional facts into later variants without making the corresponding source available in all conditions. Preserve variant versions and any source-selection decisions.

## 5. Reader method and safeguards

Recruit **at least one voluntary cold-start reader** given Corpus evidence before any Observatory memo or interpretation; seek a second independently interpreting reader if feasible under the effort ceiling. State that no participant is being asked to determine funding merit. Disclose who prepared the views. Capture consent for research notes, attribution and publication; do not publish private responses by default. Participants may decline or withdraw unpublished contributions.

Ask readers, before presenting author interpretations, to identify key observed events, unresolved questions, which claims are traceable, and what they would or would not rely on in a contemporary deliberation. Compare responses across views while recording exposure order and carryover effects. This is a small exploratory comparison, **not** causal evidence from a randomized controlled trial. Use open-ended prompts and record negative, mixed and unexpected reactions. Invite an independent check of the resulting findings before closure.

## 6. Burden ceiling and stopping

Prospectively log approximate investigator preparation/retrieval time, participant reading and response time, review work, and unmeasured effort. **Initial ceiling: 12 investigator hours and 4 cumulative invited-reader hours**, excluding a separately documented closure review. These are conservative authorization limits, not forecasts of benefit.

Stop further experiment activity and request re-review if either ceiling is reached, if a substantive source/provenance problem prevents faithful representation, or if serious authority/consent concerns arise. The probe may also stop earlier when additional variants or readers cease reducing consequential uncertainty. No expansion beyond two cases or three variants per case without an amended, reviewed agreement.

## 7. Evidence and outputs

**Observatory repo:** versioned representation variants and prompts; sample/variant method; participant-consented de-identified or attributed observations as approved; alternative interpretations; prospective effort record; explicit treatment of anchoring, false comparability and authority; independent check; closure learning record.

**Corpus repo:** existing source records remain authoritative about what is preserved. Corrections or schemas, if warranted, require separate Corpus review; AP-001 merge does not authorize them. Do not move private participant data into the Corpus.

## 8. Interpretation and closure

Check what participants actually understood and found relevant, where they traced claims, what made interpretations diverge, which representations introduced unwarranted inference or deference, and whether the observed value justified the burden. Record the role of the investigator in selecting sources, constructing views, and interpreting reader feedback.

Closure requires: both cases addressed or a documented justified stop; variants and provenance inspectable; reader observations and limitations preserved appropriately; effort log and any variance explicit; independent challenge invited; P1–P4 and parent-hypothesis implications reported; next-path decision recorded as **continue / refine / redirect / stop**. Do not equate reader preference with improved governance outcomes.

## 9. Separate prospective branch — not activated here

HTEP and the Driver identify a further question: **Can prospective evidence capture during proposal formation reduce avoidable information asymmetry while preserving proposer autonomy and independent Corpus stewardship?** This engages Pathmap Branch B and a potentially expanded domain of intervention. It remains a candidate **separately scoped and authorized** inquiry (working label AP-002), not an AP-001 phase or expected sequel.

A later proposal would need to address observed need, separation from active DRep voting interests, genuinely voluntary participation, third-party cold-start reconstruction, Corpus schema authority, independent authorization and its own burden limits. Its value need not depend on AP-001 producing favorable results.

> **Distribute work when doing so reduces burden; distribute authorization when doing so preserves the freedom to learn, reconsider and stop.**
